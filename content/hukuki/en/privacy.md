## What we process and why

Account data (email, name, sign-in records), session data (IP address, browser), profile and notification
preferences, your saved items, writing projects, uploads, practice results and Campus posts are processed to
provide the service (Law No. 6698, Art. 5/2-c). Orders, payments, invoices and credit ledger entries are
processed for sales and legal obligations (Art. 5/2-c and 5/2-ç). Consent records prove what you agreed to.
Analytics and marketing emails run only with your explicit consent. Card data never reaches Kazuist; it is
collected by the payment institution iyzico.

## Where data is stored and who receives it

Application, database, file storage and backups are in Türkiye. Recipients: hosting providers in Türkiye,
iyzico, the e-invoice integrator, our accountant, competent public authorities, the email delivery provider,
Apple for iOS purchases, and Google or Apple if you sign in with them. AI providers receive only masked text
in platform mode, routed through OpenRouter with zero data retention; with your own key (BYOK) the request goes from your device to the provider you chose.

## Court decisions

Decisions are published in anonymised form: names of parties, counsel, witnesses and first instance judges,
identity numbers, addresses and similar identifiers are masked. Names are never indexed for search.

## Your rights

Under Art. 11 of Law No. 6698 you may ask whether your data is processed, request information, correction,
erasure, notification of third parties, object to solely automated decisions against you, and claim damages.
Use the [application form](/hukuki/basvuru). We respond free of charge within thirty days (Art. 13/2).
