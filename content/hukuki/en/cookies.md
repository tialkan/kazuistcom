> TASLAK, Tarık onayı bekliyor. Version date: 2 November 2026.
> This English text is for information only. The binding version is Turkish: [Çerez politikası](/hukuki/cerezler).

- Necessary cookies: the session cookie and short-lived sign-in verification cookies (5 to 10 minutes), all HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax. No consent needed.
- Analytics: Umami, self-hosted, no personal data, only if you accept.
- No advertising or third-party tracking cookies.
- If you enter your own AI key (BYOK), it is kept in your browser for the session only, or persistently if
  you choose "remember on this device". It is never stored on our servers.

Analytics is not active yet, so no cookie choice is asked and no preference cookie is set. Once analytics is enabled, "Accept" and "Reject" will have equal weight and you can change your choice via "Cookie preferences" in the footer.
