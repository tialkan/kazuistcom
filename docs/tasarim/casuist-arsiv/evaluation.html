Evaluation protocol

Method first. Numbers only after measurement.

This page describes what will be measured and how. It does not publish provisional performance figures. Results will appear only after the protocol, corpus and independent evaluation are complete.

01 / Citation fidelity

A citation must support the proposition attributed to it.

Evaluation begins at proposition level. An annotator sees the proposition, the cited source span and the legal context in which the source is offered.

The test asks whether the passage entails or otherwise supports the proposition, whether the source is accurately represented, and whether the link remains valid in the relevant jurisdiction and time frame.

02 / Gold standard

Qualified legal annotators create the reference set.

The benchmark records the proposition, candidate source spans, authority status and an error label. Annotation guidance defines each label before production work begins.

A calibration round is followed by independent annotation. Disagreements are retained, measured and adjudicated under a recorded protocol. The benchmark is versioned with the legal corpus.

03 / Agreement

Disagreement is data, not noise to conceal.

Inter-annotator agreement is reported with the sampling frame and label distribution. Difficult classes are reviewed separately rather than compressed into one aggregate score.

Changes to guidance, corpus or thresholds create a new evaluation version. Results from different versions are not presented as directly comparable without qualification.

04 / Independence

The evaluator must be able to disagree with the builder.

The team that implements retrieval and generation does not alone decide whether the output passes. The research partner leads benchmark design, annotation governance and error analysis.

This separation makes negative results usable. It also prevents engineering choices from silently redefining the test they are expected to pass.

Error taxonomy

E1

Fabricated citation

The cited source or identifying detail cannot be verified.

E2

Real but non-controlling

The source exists but does not carry the asserted authority for the proposition.

E3

No longer in force

The source has been repealed, superseded or displaced for the relevant time.

E4

Out-of-scope application

The source is applied beyond the persons, facts, procedure or legal issue it covers.

Current publication status

No performance number is published. The first public result will identify the corpus version, sampling frame, annotation protocol, evaluator and uncertainty around each reported measure.
