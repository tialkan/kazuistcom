Eurostars Call 12 partner search

Lead the legal evaluation, not a data hand-off.

Kulular Teknoloji is preparing a Eurostars Call 12 proposal for Casuist. We are looking for a European university, research organisation or R&D-performing SME to co-design and lead the scientific evaluation.

Submission deadline4 March 2027, 14:00 Brussels time
Official call page ↗

Target programme. This project has not received Eurostars or TÜBİTAK funding.

Eligibility and funding route

  • Türkiye is listed as a Eurostars country for Call 12.
  • The consortium must be led by an innovative SME from a Eurostars country and include at least two independent entities from at least two Eurostars countries.
  • Turkish participation is planned through TÜBİTAK's 1709 EUREKA Eurostars route. National criteria remain subject to the responsible funding body.
  • The official Eureka call page is the controlling source for dates, international eligibility and country funding status.

Call opens on 17 December 2026. Eureka states that country funding information is still being confirmed and may change before opening. Each partner should verify its national funding route early.

WP / 01–06

Draft work package structure

The partner leads three scientific work packages. Detailed tasks, effort and budget are negotiated before drafting. No duration or person-month figure is fixed on this site.

WP1Project management and consortium coordinationKulular
WP2Legal requirements, jurisdictional scope and evaluation frameworkPartner
WP3Benchmark corpus and gold-standard annotationPartner
WP4Retrieval, grounding and provenance engineKulular
WP5Validation, benchmarking and error analysisPartner
WP6Pilot integration, commercialisation and disseminationShared

A strong partner can answer yes to several of these

  • Can define a bounded jurisdiction, court hierarchy and document set for evaluation.
  • Has qualified legal researchers who can design and supervise annotation.
  • Can lead benchmark methodology, agreement measurement and error analysis.
  • Can host or lawfully access a suitable legal corpus and document its licensing basis.
  • Can contribute to peer-reviewed dissemination and a credible pilot setting.
  • Can confirm access to national Eurostars funding or a self-funding route.

IP / Draft boundary

Proposed intellectual property boundary

Each party retains its background intellectual property. Kulular retains foreground ownership of the core platform software.

The evaluation methodology, benchmark dataset and resulting publications are proposed as jointly owned. The partner retains research and teaching rights in its field and a defined commercial route.

This boundary is negotiated before proposal drafting begins. It is not deferred to the consortium agreement stage.
