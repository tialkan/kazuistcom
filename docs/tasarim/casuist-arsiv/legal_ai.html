Legal text

AI policy

Last updated 11 September 2026

01

Intended role

Casuist is being designed as a decision-support tool for qualified legal professionals. It does not determine outcomes. The user remains responsible for professional judgement and use.

02

Provenance requirement

Provenance is a product requirement, not an optional feature. Each generated proposition must be connected to the source passage presented as its support.

03

Abstention

The system is designed to withhold a proposition when support does not meet the defined threshold. An unsupported status is preferable to a fluent completion without an inspectable basis.

04

EU AI Act assessment

No risk category is claimed in advance for every deployment. Classification must be assessed against the intended purpose, users, context and actual function of each deployment. Uses connected to the administration of justice require a separate assessment.

05

Corpora and data

Every corpus used for development or evaluation must have a documented legal basis, a licensing review and processing controls consistent with applicable KVKK and GDPR obligations.

06

Human oversight

Oversight is meaningful only when the professional can inspect the basis, challenge the output and decline it. An approval click alone is not treated as control.
