Four mechanisms

Designed around the failure, not the interface.

Each mechanism begins with a specific failure and ends with a behaviour that can be inspected or measured. A polished answer is not the unit of trust. The proposition and its support are.

01

Proposition-level grounding

01Proposition
02Source span
03Visible link
Problem
A bibliography at the end cannot show which source span supports which sentence.
Design
Each proposition is bound to the exact source passage it rests on. The passage opens beside the proposition.
02

Controlling versus cited

01Jurisdiction
02Force
03Scope
Problem
A decision may exist and still be non-controlling for the question at hand.
Design
Authority is assessed for jurisdiction, current force and scope. Cited and controlling sources remain distinct.
03

Abstention

01Evidence test
02Threshold
03Withhold
Problem
A model tends to complete a plausible sentence when the supporting material is weak.
Design
When the support threshold is not met, the proposition is withheld and marked unsupported.
04

Independent evaluation

01Gold standard
02Blind test
03Error review
Problem
A result measured only by its own engineering team is not enough for professional reliance.
Design
A separate team compares outputs against a gold standard annotated by qualified legal experts.
