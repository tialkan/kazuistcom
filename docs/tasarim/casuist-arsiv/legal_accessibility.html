Accessibility statement

Last updated 11 September 2026

01

Commitment

We aim for this website to conform to WCAG 2.1 Level AA. The site is designed for keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable contrast, text resizing and reduced motion preferences.

02

Known limits

The archived previous site at /eski predates this statement and may not meet the same standard. The current public pages use semantic headings and do not require pointer-only interaction.

03

Feedback

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, email [email protected] with the page address and a short description. We will review the issue and provide an accessible alternative where reasonably possible.
