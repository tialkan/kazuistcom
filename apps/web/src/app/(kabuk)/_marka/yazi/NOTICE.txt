Fonts for the OpenGraph card (opengraph-image.tsx)

These files are used only at build time to draw the share card; they are not served to visitors.
All are licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1. The licence texts and the original
modification notes are in apps/web/public/fonts (NOTICE.txt and OFL-*.txt).

SourceSerif4-Regular.ttf, SourceSerif4-Semibold.ttf
  From apps/web/public/fonts/SourceSerif4.woff2, weight pinned at 400 and 600.
KzSans-Medium.ttf
  From apps/web/public/fonts/KzSans.woff2 (modified IBM Plex Sans, renamed under OFL section 3),
  weight pinned at 500.
KzMono-Regular.ttf
  From apps/web/public/fonts/KzMono-Regular.woff2 (modified IBM Plex Mono, renamed under OFL section 3).

Modification: fontTools.varLib.instancer pinned the variable axes, then the fonts were saved as plain
TrueType because next/og (Satori) does not read WOFF2. The glyph subset is unchanged.
